# TrustBooker product constitution

## Mission
Build a global horizontal services marketplace where trust is inspectable and marketplace incentives do not reward spam or pay-to-win visibility.

## Non-negotiable product rules
1. Core participation is free for buyers and providers.
2. No lead fees. A provider should not pay merely to receive an enquiry.
3. No paid ranking. Sponsored money cannot silently overwrite relevance or reputation.
4. Primary marketplace revenue is a 10% success commission on eligible completed transactions.
5. Verification claims must be granular: identity, business, booking and review verification are separate signals.
6. A verified-booking review requires an eligible booking record; ordinary testimonials cannot inherit that badge.
7. Financial events are append-only/auditable in the operational model.
8. Market activation is explicit per country; launch gates must exist for payments, payouts, disputes, support and legal readiness.
9. Safety systems may reduce distribution, require additional verification or restrict participation when evidence justifies it.
10. Never fabricate marketplace activity, ratings, booking counts or trust signals in production.
